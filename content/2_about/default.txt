## Why "StackWerk"

*Werk* is German for work, and for a works: the place where things get made. StackWerk is that place for my apps. Each one starts as something I wanted to use myself, and gets built properly, with the tools its platform was designed for.

## What I make

The first app is (link: apps/winston text: Winston), a native Windows 11 client for Mastodon. It's built with WinUI 3 and .NET, uses the Fluent design of Windows 11, and keeps your accounts on your PC. More apps are on the bench.

## How I work

- **Native first.** Apps use their platform's own toolkit, so they fit in with everything else on your device.
- **No tracking.** There's no StackWerk account, no analytics and no ads in the apps.
- **Small, frequent releases.** Every change is built and tested automatically, and released when it passes.

## Get in touch

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on the app's GitHub page. For Winston, that's (link: https://github.com/JamesNMartin/Winston/issues text: github.com/JamesNMartin/Winston).
