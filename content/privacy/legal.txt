## This website

- **No cookies for visitors.** The site doesn't set cookies when you browse it. (Editors who sign in to manage the site get a session cookie.)
- **No analytics, ads or trackers.** There are no third-party scripts, embeds or tracking pixels.
- **Fonts and images are served from this site,** so loading a page doesn't contact anyone else.
- **Server logs.** Like any web server, the one hosting stackwerk.co records requests, including your IP address, browser and the page you asked for, to keep the site running and secure.

## Getting the apps

StackWerk's apps come from the Microsoft Store. Store links take you to Microsoft's website or the Microsoft Store app, where the (link: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement text: Microsoft Privacy Statement) applies.
