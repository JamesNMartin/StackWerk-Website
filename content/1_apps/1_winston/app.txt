Nativeheadline: At home on Windows 11

----

Nativetext: Winston uses the current Fluent controls and the parts of Windows you already know.

----

Nativeitems: Mica and Acrylic backdrops, Light and dark themes, Windows' accent colour or your own, Toast notifications, The Windows share sheet, An icon in the notification area, Text recognition built into Windows, Sign-ins protected by Windows (DPAPI), Sharp on every monitor (per-monitor DPI), Follows Windows' animation setting, Keyboard shortcuts throughout

----

Privacyheadline: Between you and your server

----

Privacytext: Winston talks directly to the Mastodon servers you sign in to. There's no StackWerk account, no analytics and no tracking. Your settings, accounts and drafts stay on your PC, and sign-in tokens are encrypted by Windows for your user alone.

Faq:

- 
  question: How much does Winston cost?
  answer: Nothing. Winston is a free download.
- 
  question: Which download do I need?
  answer: >
    Most Windows PCs need the **64-bit (x64)**
    setup. PCs with a Snapdragon or other ARM
    processor need **ARM64**, and 32-bit Windows
    needs **x86**. Not sure? Open Settings →
    System → About and look at "System type".
- 
  question: Why does Windows warn me when I run the setup?
  answer: >
    Winston isn't code-signed yet, so Windows
    SmartScreen doesn't recognise it. Choose
    **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- 
  question: How do I update Winston?
  answer: >
    Download the latest setup from this page and
    run it. It updates Winston in place and keeps
    your accounts and settings.
- 
  question: Can I use Winston without installing it?
  answer: >
    Yes. Each release has portable zips that run
    from any folder. Unzip one and start
    `Winston.exe`.
- 
  question: Does it work with my server?
  answer: >
    Winston works with any Mastodon server. Some
    features need a recent version: grouped
    notifications need Mastodon 4.3, quote posts
    4.5 and collections 4.6. Posting in Markdown
    or HTML works on servers that accept it, such
    as glitch-soc, GoToSocial and Akkoma.
- 
  question: How do I uninstall it?
  answer: >
    Open Settings → Apps, find Winston in the
    list and choose Uninstall. Your settings
    and accounts are kept in
    `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Winston` in case you come
    back; delete that folder to remove them too.
- 
  question: Where do I report a bug or suggest a feature?
  answer: >
    Open an issue on
    (link: https://github.com/JamesNMartin/Winston/issues text: Winston's GitHub page).
